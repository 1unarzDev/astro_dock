
--- SITL Run Command ---
Tools/autotest/sim_vehicle.py -v "$VEHICLE" $SITL_EXTRA_ARGS

--- Stop SITL ---
Use Ctrl+C in its terminal. Inspect conflicts with: sudo lsof -i :<port>

--- ROS side (run in the core/cuda development container, not this image) ---
ros2 launch <your_package> <robot>.launch.py sim:=true   (your stack's launch)
The SITL image simulates the FCU; it does not own physical PWM pins.
Use sim:=false only for deliberate real-robot operation in the development
container. Your stack's own help lists its launch arguments and hardware
commands.
