
--- SITL Run Command ---
Tools/autotest/sim_vehicle.py -v "$VEHICLE" $SITL_EXTRA_ARGS

--- Stop SITL ---
Use Ctrl+C in its terminal. Inspect conflicts with: sudo lsof -i :<port>

--- ROS side (run in the core/cuda development container, not this image) ---
ros2 launch mhseals_nav robot.launch.py sim:=true
The SITL image simulates the FCU; it does not own physical ODROID PWM pins.
Use sim:=false only for deliberate real-boat operation in the development
container. Run its help command for sensors_ignore, keyboard_control, shared
hardware YAML, bags, MAVROS, and camera commands.
